CORE COMBAT SPECIFICATION
=========================

BATTLE FORMAT
-------------
- Separate generic Battle scene; normal battles should not require bespoke .tscn files.
- Exploration scene is stopped while Battle scene is active.
- Battle receives setup data: enemy list, background, optional BGM etc.
- Default battle BGM is "Basic Battle BGM 1" (the provided 8-bit track if present in project).
- Normal enemy count: 1–3.
- Internal enemy storage MUST NOT hardcode a maximum of 3. Boss/special encounters may exceed it.
- Normal placement:
  1 enemy: center
  2 enemies: left/right; initial target = left
  3 enemies: left/center/right; initial target = center
- Dead enemy slots remain empty; do not compact positions.
- On target death, select next living target using a stable left-to-right rule.

BATTLE STATES
-------------
Minimum battle-level states:
STARTING / ACTIVE / VICTORY / DEFEAT

STARTING:
- approximately 0.5 s.
- enemy attack timers do not advance.
- player CAN input/cast during this short opening.
- BGM begins immediately.
- no "BATTLE START" text required.

ACTIVE:
- all normal real-time systems run.

VICTORY:
- triggered immediately when all enemies are dead.
- cancel enemy attacks/AI.
- player input locks.
- BGM stops immediately.
- remain on result/retry state for current test implementation.

DEFEAT:
- triggered immediately at player HP <= 0.
- player input locks.
- enemy AI stops.
- in-progress player spell presentation is cancelled.
- BGM stops immediately.
- darken screen and show DEFEAT + Retry.
- no exit button required in current test battle.

PLAYER
------
- no movement in combat.
- controls are rune casting, target selection, cancellation, and Algiz parry.
- future dodge/jump exists conceptually but MUST NOT be implemented now.
- max HP initial: 100.
- current HP initial: 100 in test battle.
- Magic Power initial: 10.
- Magic Power is NOT mana and is NOT consumed.
- no defense stat for now.
- spells are unlimited.
- future equipment may modify HP/Magic Power/resistance/casting/parry, but current equipment modifiers must remain disconnected from active combat.

DAMAGE FORMULA
--------------
Current temporary Magic Power formula:
  mp_scaled_damage = base_damage * (1 + magic_power / 100.0)

Then apply relevant spell/perfect/resistance logic.
Final displayed/applied integer damage is FLOORed, not rounded.

The calculation should live in a centralized/resolvable function so the formula can be changed later without rewriting spells.

PERFECT:
- each PERFECT in the currently built combo increments perfect_count.
- final spell damage multiplier = 1.5 ^ perfect_count.
- examples: one P = x1.5; two P = x2.25.
- do not record which rune caused the P; only the count is needed.
- no ordinary random critical-hit system.

PLAYER HIT
----------
Normal damaging hit:
- apply damage.
- clear active casting.
- clear entire built combo.
- clear perfect_count.
- clear fusion display.
- cancel active parry stance.
- hitstun: 0.2 s.
- invulnerability: total 0.6 s from the original damaging hit.
- after 0.2 s, player may cast/parry during the remaining ~0.4 s of invulnerability.
- target selection remains unchanged.
- ordinary hit visual: light screen shake, player damage number, HP reaction.

INVULNERABILITY:
- an attack can still geometrically/logically hit during i-frames, but damage = 0.
- such an invulnerable hit must NOT clear the player's combo/casting.
- it DOES cancel an active parry stance.
- most importantly: it MUST NOT restart or extend the 0.6 s invulnerability timer.
- avoid accidental infinite i-frame extension from rapid attacks.

INPUT EVENT PRIORITY / SAME-FRAME CASES
---------------------------------------
Explicitly resolve competing events. Important intended outcomes:
- a spell whose Enter activation is accepted in the same frame as incoming damage is considered launched; then the player can be hit.
- successful parry beats the corresponding hit.
- death supersedes continued interaction.
- Algiz has higher input priority than X if both are newly pressed in exactly the same frame (as specified in Q&A).
- avoid implementation-order-dependent behavior.

A sensible explicit processing policy should preserve:
spell activation acceptance > parry resolution > damaging hit > Algiz > X > other inputs,
while death checks terminate subsequent player actions.

BATTLE RESULT / STATS
---------------------
Prepare BattleResult-style runtime data for future integration:
- victory
- remaining_hp
- defeated_enemy_ids
- battle duration
- perfect count/statistics
- parry count
- hit count
- spell-use counts
No reward UI is needed now.
XP, level, loot and rune drops are NOT part of this implementation.

RETURN TO EXPLORATION
---------------------
Architecture should support returning to the originating exploration scene and carrying remaining HP/results, but the test battle may simply wait for Retry.
