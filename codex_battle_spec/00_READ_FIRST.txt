DESIGN PRINCIPLE
----------------
This is NOT a turn-based command RPG. Combat is a real-time technical duel inspired primarily by Sekiro-style pressure:
- enemies continue attacking while the player casts;
- spells consume no mana;
- parry consumes no resource;
- player limitations are TIME, INPUT ACCURACY, and ENEMY PRESSURE;
- long combos are stronger but expose the player to more casting/MISS/interruption risk;
- there is NO ultimate/special gauge;
- large openings are earned by handling enemy attacks, especially parries.
