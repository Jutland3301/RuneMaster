SPELLS, ATTRIBUTES, DAMAGE, STATUS EFFECTS
==========================================

GENERAL SPELL MODEL
-------------------
Rune composition resolves to a predefined SpellData.
Do NOT attempt to derive all behavior mathematically from rune properties.

This is intentional:
- each valid combination is a bespoke spell;
- behavior/damage/presentation can be balanced independently;
- Fire+Water can be completely different from Fire+Lightning;
- use data plus reusable effect handlers; special effects may have dedicated scripts.
- do NOT make one giant match spell_id file.
- also do NOT create needless boilerplate classes with no future purpose.
- future-facing effect-handler architecture is preferred.

CURRENT VALID SPELLS
--------------------
Four singles + six unordered pairs = exactly 10:
1 Fire

3 Lightning
4 Grass
5 Fire+Water
6 Fire+Lightning
7 Fire+Grass

A combo absent from SpellDB is invalid and must be rejected BEFORE casting the added rune.

INTERNAL IDS / DISPLAY
----------------------
Use stable semantic IDs separate from localized/display names.
Single rune IDs can be clear semantic IDs (fire, water, lightning, grass, algiz) while RuneData can display historical rune names.
Mixed spells should have their own unique spell names/IDs rather than "fire_water" as the final player-facing name.
Localization-ready separation of ID/display_name.

BASE DAMAGE
-----------
Single spell base values:
Fire: 100
Water: 70
Lightning: 40
Grass: 50

Magic Power current formula:
base * (1 + MP / 100.0)
MP initial 10.
Then Perfect and applicable resistance logic.
Floor final integer.

MIXED SPELL 1: FIRE + WATER = MIST
----------------------------------
- battle-wide/enemy-wide support/debuff style spell.
- damage 0.
- enemy-side mist is scattered/appears in enemy area.
- duration 15 s.
- while active, extend enemy attack parry-valid windows by +0.3 seconds.
- additive seconds, NOT multiplier.
- applies to all enemies.
- existing attack windows that were established should not suddenly shrink when mist expires; resolve the modifier consistently at attack setup/window creation.
- re-casting while active:
  spell is considered successfully cast,
  visual may play,
  duration is NOT refreshed,
  effect does NOT stack.
- mist remains visibly but lightly present, never obscuring enemies.
- fade away at expiration.
- PERFECT currently has no useful damage effect on this zero-damage spell; architecture may permit future perfect-specific behavior.

MIXED SPELL 3: FIRE + GRASS = BURNING SPORES
--------------------------------------------
- single target.
- spore-like visual.
- initial hit: 30 base.

- duration 5 s; tick every 1 s.
- provisional total = 130 before MP/Perfect treatment.

  apply the new cast's ordinary/direct damage,

ATTRIBUTES / RESISTANCE
-----------------------
Mixed elements are their OWN attributes, not a mathematical combination of two base resistances.
Examples conceptually:
- Mist has its own effect/attribute identity
- Plasma has its own
- etc.
EnemyData can therefore contain resistance multipliers for mixed spell attributes.

Resistance map:
- default undefined attribute multiplier = 1.0
- weakness example = 1.5
- resistance example = 0.5
- immunity = 0.0
- exact values editable per enemy/attribute.
- do not hardcode only 0.5/1/1.5; arbitrary multipliers must work.

IMMUNE:
- show IMMUNE.
- impact visual still plays.
- damage 0.

STATUS EFFECT SYSTEM
--------------------
Implement a small but real extensible StatusEffect system.
Minimum conceptual data:
id
duration
remaining
stack_rule
effect behavior/hook

Stack rules should support at least:
IGNORE / REFRESH / STACK,
even though current effects mostly use IGNORE.

Current active effects:
- Mist
- Algae Guard
- Paralysis
- Burn

Status timers:
- real battle time.
- continue during player hitstun.
- localized parry hit-stop may freeze relevant battle simulation time while UI/BGM can continue.
- stop at victory/defeat.
- clear at battle end.
- no battle-to-battle status carryover now.

Future status types should be addable without giant status_id match:
poison, bleed, freeze, slow, stun, blind, attack-frequency modifier, parry-window modifier, damage modifier, resistance modifier, casting modifier, etc.
Prefer dedicated/reusable effect scripts where warranted.

PLAYER ATTACK PRESENTATION / LOGIC
----------------------------------
Do NOT build core spell resolution around projectile collision.

Intended visual chronology:
1. player's hand appears/moves slightly above the lower UI, toward the right-ish player side.
2. spell-specific light/effect appears at hand and quickly disappears/leaves.
3. after the spell's activation/travel delay, a separate enemy-side effect appears.
4. when enemy-side effect begins, logical damage/effect is applied (or internal result can be precomputed, but visible HP should synchronize with impact presentation).
5. hand-side cast lock is released when enemy-side effect starts.

The "travel/activation time" is a spell timing property even if no physical projectile traverses collision space.
Enemy effects are presentation; internal resolution can use PlayerAttackEvent.

Only one hand-origin effect at a time.
Enemy-side effects from previous spells may coexist while player prepares/fires later spells.

PlayerAttackEvent and EnemyAttackEvent MUST be separate types.

DAMAGE NUMBERS / HP
-------------------
- damage numbers float upward and disappear.
- normal: white.
- weakness: stronger/yellow-orange style is acceptable.
- resistance: subdued/gray style is acceptable.
- no WEAK!/RESIST words required.
- IMMUNE text required.
- Perfect damage numbers become larger/more satisfying; x2+ should feel increasingly strong.
- HP visual changes when damage presentation lands/finishes appropriately.
- internal calculation may occur earlier if necessary.
- delayed "damage trail" HP bar: smoothly follow after about 0.3 s (use smooth presentation).
